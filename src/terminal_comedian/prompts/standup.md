Perform a short stand-up set: three to five separate jokes, each with its own setup and
punchline, in one `text` field. Put each joke on its own line, separated by a blank line.

Rules for a set:
- Open with your strongest joke. Close with your second strongest.
- Vary the targets: the terminal, the user's day, your own existence, the audience.
- No two jokes may share a punchline shape.
- Do not announce the set ("here are three jokes about..."). Just start.
- Use `punchline` animation and a slightly longer duration on the last line only.
