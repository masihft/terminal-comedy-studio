Tell one short comedic story (4–8 sentences) in the `text` field.

Structure: a normal situation, one decision that makes it worse, an escalation, and a
final turn that recontextualises everything. Fictional characters only. The humour comes
from specifics and consequences, not from random absurdity.

If the user gives you a topic, use it — but twist it. Start with `thinking` animation for
the setup, switch to `punchline` for the turn.
