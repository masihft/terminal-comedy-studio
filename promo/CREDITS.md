# Promo reel credits

- **Video:** made with [Remotion](https://www.remotion.dev). The mascot, its animations and
  the pixel lettering are drawn from the project's own pixel data
  (`src/terminal_comedian/character/frames.py`, `ui/pixels.py`); the jokes and the roast are
  the comedian's real offline lines.
- **Music:** "Pixel Party", supplied by the project owner.
- **Typing sounds:** the "Mechanical Keyboard" theme from
  [6m1w/claude-sound-fx](https://github.com/6m1w/claude-sound-fx) (MIT).
- **Other sound effects** (pop, ding, laugh, rimshot, whoosh, tada): synthesised for this reel.
- **Persian font:** Abar (AbarMid FaNum).
